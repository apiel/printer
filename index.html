Margins (mm)
Padding (mm)

Margins are blank space printed around the page, so printing starts that far in: with a width of 210 and a left margin of 10, the first dot of the page is printed 10 mm from the edge (they are not shown on screen). Padding is the space new items keep from the page edge (the faint dashed line).
