Page size

Pick the size in the top bar. Width, length and the left and right margins are in the strip below it when nothing is selected. The rest is here.

Margins top / bottom (mm)
Padding (mm)

Margins are the edge the printer cannot reach: they are not part of the page and are not printed (a 58 mm roll with 5 mm margins gives a 48 mm page). Padding is the space new items keep from the edge (the faint dashed line).
